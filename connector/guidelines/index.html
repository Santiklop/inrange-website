Connector for Claude

The OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, paragraph by paragraph, inside Claude

Claude can search the text of the Guidelines, quote the paragraphs that answer your question word for word with their numbers, and check paragraph numbers that it or you cite.

What it does

The connector gives Claude the OECD’s own text as this site publishes it under OECD TPG by paragraph: the 2022 Guidelines and the Amount B annex. Claude decides when to use it. A typical exchange:

YouHow should a cash pool leader be rewarded under the OECD Guidelines? Quote the relevant paragraphs.

ClaudeAnswers the question and quotes the paragraphs of Chapter X on rewarding the cash pool leader word for word, with their numbers, the OECD citation and a link to each paragraph on this site.

It is read-only and free. It needs no account, and nothing you ask is stored.

Add it to Claude

The connector’s address is:

https://tpg.inrange.nl/mcp

In Claude on the web, desktop or phone

  1. Open Customize > Connectors.
  2. Select Add, then Add custom connector.
  3. Enter a name, for example OECD TP Guidelines, and the address above. Select Continue.
  4. For authentication choose No sign-in, then select Add. Claude may first say that it could not determine how the server signs in. That is expected, because the connector has no login.
  5. In a chat, open the + menu, choose Connectors and switch it on.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, an Owner adds it once for the organisation under Organization settings > Connectors. Members then find it under Customize > Connectors. The Free plan allows one custom connector.

When the connector is listed in Claude’s directory, you will also find it there as OECD TP Guidelines by inRange.

In Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http oecd-tp-guidelines https://tpg.inrange.nl/mcp

What to ask

You do not have to mention the connector. When it is switched on, Claude uses it where a question calls for it. Some questions that work well:

  • How should a cash pool leader be rewarded under the OECD Guidelines? Quote the paragraphs.
  • Quote paragraph 1.142 of the Transfer Pricing Guidelines.
  • Check the OECD paragraph numbers cited in this memo.
  • Show me the Amount B pricing matrix.
  • Which sections does Chapter IX have?

The three tools

ToolWhat it returns
search_oecd_textThe passages closest to a question: up to three word for word, each with its paragraph number, footnotes and link, then up to eight more as single lines. A paragraph number returns that paragraph first. When nothing in the text is about the question, it says so.
get_oecd_paragraphsParagraphs by number, word for word: one, a range, a list, an annex paragraph, an Amount B paragraph, an annex example or a glossary term, at most 15 at a time. Each comes with its footnotes, the paragraphs it refers to and that refer to it, and links to the worked examples on this site that illustrate it.
list_oecd_sectionsThe parts of the text with their paragraph counts, or the section headings of one part with the paragraph numbers under each.

All three only read. None of them can change anything, anywhere.

What is covered

  • OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines 2022: the Preface, the Glossary, Chapters I to X with their annexes, and the Annex to the Guidelines on monitoring procedures. About 2,000 numbered paragraphs.
  • Amount B: the annex to Chapter IV on baseline distribution activities, with Appendices A and B and the two statements on covered and qualifying jurisdictions, from the Consolidated Report on Amount B (revised version of May 2026).

Not included: the June 2026 discussion draft of Chapter VII; the Foreword, abbreviations and Appendix of the 2022 edition; and the Foreword, Executive Summary, Introduction and Model Competent Authority Agreement of the Consolidated Report on Amount B.

The text is the OECD’s, word for word, and every answer ends with the citation of the publication it quotes. Where a worked example on this site illustrates a paragraph, the answer links to it. Those examples are inRange’s paraphrases for learning; the examples connector serves them.

Sources: OECD (2022), OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises and Tax Administrations 2022, OECD Publishing, Paris, https://doi.org/10.1787/0e655865-en; and OECD (2025), Consolidated Report on Amount B: Inclusive Framework on BEPS, OECD/G20 Base Erosion and Profit Shifting Project, OECD Publishing, Paris, https://doi.org/10.1787/182b47ad-en, licence CC BY 4.0.

Limits

  • Answers are kept short. A search returns up to three passages in full and a few more as single lines, and a request for paragraphs returns at most 15 at a time.
  • When called directly, for example from Claude Code, one address may make 30 requests a minute.
  • The text follows the pages on this site and is updated when they are.

What it is not

inRange is an independent transfer pricing advisory firm. This connector is not affiliated with or endorsed by the OECD. The authoritative text is the OECD’s own publications. Nothing the connector returns is tax advice.

Privacy

inRange stores nothing about your requests: no questions, no search terms, no addresses. The privacy policy has the details.
