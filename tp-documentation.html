Transfer pricing documentation · Our approach

Three ways to produce your transfer pricing documentation.

Multinational groups producing TP documentation across many entities, jurisdictions and years have three credible paths to get the work done. This page compares them — and sets out how we deliver the one we recommend.

What this page is about

How the work is actually done, how the team is shaped, what it costs in time and effort, and — importantly — what stays in your hands after the engagement ends.

Section 04

How ownership transfers

The exoskeleton is built to be handed over. Over the course of the engagement, your team takes on more of the work, and we step back to review, edge cases, and controversy.

Year 1

We run the exoskeleton

inRangeYou

We operate the agents end-to-end. Your team provides inputs, reviews milestones, and receives finished documentation. The backlog comes down; the workflow gets sharper with every file produced.

Year 2

Co-pilot

inRangeYou

Whoever you nominate starts operating the agents alongside us. We shift to review, edge cases, and policy work. The toolchain documentation gets formalised.

Year 3 +

You wear the exoskeleton

inRangeYou

Your team runs the workflow in-house, using a simple AI subscription (whatever is best-in-class at the time). We remain on call for review, refinements, and any controversy work. Human involvement keeps decreasing as the agents improve.
