Connector for Claude

Privacy policy

This policy covers the connector OECD Transfer Pricing Examples by inRange, which runs at mcp.inrange.nl. In short: it answers questions from a fixed set of public examples, and inRange stores nothing about the people who ask.

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

inRange Solutions B.V., Amsterdam, the Netherlands, operates the connector and is the controller for the limited processing described here. You can reach us at info@inrange.nl.

What the connector receives

When Claude uses the connector, it sends one of three small requests:

  • a few search words that Claude chose to describe your question, with an optional chapter and number of results;
  • the identifier of an example, such as IX-6;
  • a request for the list of examples, with an optional chapter.

The connector does not receive your conversation, your files, your name, your email address or anything about your Claude account. It has no login and sets no cookies.

Like every internet service, it also sees the network address a request comes from. When you use the connector through Claude's apps, requests come from Anthropic's servers, so that address is Anthropic's and not yours. When a program on your own computer calls it directly, for example Claude Code, the address is the one your connection uses.

What inRange stores

Nothing. The connector keeps no record of search words, identifiers, addresses or times. Its own logging is switched off. It has no database and no analytics of its own. Each request is answered from the examples that are built into it and then forgotten.

Our hosting provider

The connector runs on the network of Cloudflare, Inc., which processes requests on our behalf in order to deliver and protect the service. In doing so Cloudflare handles technical data such as network addresses, and it produces aggregate figures such as the number of requests.

To prevent misuse, Cloudflare counts the requests from one network address for up to one minute. Requests that arrive from Anthropic's servers are not counted in this way.

Cloudflare operates worldwide, so a request may be handled outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards in Cloudflare's data processing terms. Cloudflare's own handling of data is described in the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Claude and Anthropic

Your use of Claude, including what you type and what Claude does with the connector's answers, is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy, not by this one. inRange has no access to your conversations.

Why this processing is allowed

The only personal data involved is the network address, and only for as long as it takes to answer a request and to apply the rate limit. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a public information service and protecting it from misuse (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).

Sharing and selling

We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone. Apart from Cloudflare as host, no third party is involved. The connector shows no advertising.

How long data is kept

inRange keeps nothing, so there is nothing to delete. Cloudflare's short-lived request counts expire within a minute. Any operational records Cloudflare keeps for its own network follow Cloudflare's policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to your personal data, for its correction or deletion, and you may object to its processing. Because we store nothing that identifies you, we will usually have nothing to give you or to delete, but you are welcome to ask at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes to this policy

If the connector starts to work differently, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and the date above will change with it.
