    Method

    Mark-up on total costs (FCMU) = operating profit (EBIT) ÷ total costs, with total costs = operating revenue less EBIT. The weighted average 2022–2024 is the sum of EBIT over the sum of total costs for the years available (at least two). Quartiles as Excel QUARTILE (inclusive), on the same values as the workbooks.

    Back and middle office includes by default the 27 companies found only by its strategies B and C, which use the exports of the IT development and strategic management searches; all 27 were rejected there for their activities. No company is accepted in more than one set.

    Company names are shortened to their first letters and legal form.
